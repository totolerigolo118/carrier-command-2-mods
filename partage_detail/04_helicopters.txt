HELICOPTERS ON THE CARRIER (and the "second elevator" study)
============================================================

Razorbill = light rotor (e_game_object_type 12), Petrel = heavy rotor (14, it can carry a vehicle: it drops it just before
touching down). Same function as aircraft: docking_manager_air::update, with a helicopter-specific part (test `get_is_rotor`,
virtual 0xa0, at 0x1001ad3e1). Offsets of the manager: 01_mac_binary_and_patching_method.txt section 7.
Ship frame: x to starboard, z to the bow, deck at y = 20.


1. HOW THE GAME LANDS A HELICOPTER (stock)
------------------------------------------
The manager has ONE slot per stage (a slot holds the id of a single vehicle): queue 0x48 (aircraft AND helicopters, one common
queue), runway 0x80, elevator 0x7c, crane 0x78, rotor take-off 0x84 (used by launches only).

 1. WAIT: helicopters hold a fixed point behind the ship, x = -100, z = -240 - 60 x rank, altitude 80
    (0x1001af4af ... 0x1001af551). The first one gets the runway when it is within 100 m of the first place (0x1001adadc;
    constants (-100, -240) at 0x10286b880, 100^2 at 0x10286998c) AND the runway is free.
 2. APPROACH (runway state 3, helicopter part 0x1001ad3ef ... 0x1001ad61f then 0x1001b102e): the helicopter aims at a point on the
    runway axis 50 m ahead of itself (at most z = 52 = runway_start + 120) and descends according to the distance to the MIDDLE of the
    runway (z = 7): target height = 0.5 x (7 - z), clamped to [-2, 80] m. Within 20 m of the middle: gear down, landing state.
    The autopilot corrects the position error at 0.1 per metre, hence only ~5 m/s toward the point and ~1:00 of approach.
 3. TOUCHDOWN (0x1001b1f5c ... 0x1001b2058, common with aircraft): speed relative to the ship < 10 m/s and all wheels in contact. The
    helicopter is attached to the ship (its future hangar slot, +0x90), state -> 5. No "rails" for helicopters (state 4 is the aircraft one).
 4. DECK TAXI in 3 legs, speed restarting from 0 on each leg (so three brakings): state 5 from the touchdown point (z ~ 7..30) to taxi_a
    (0, -44); state 6 taxi_a -> lift_upper_wait (12, -32); wait until the elevator is up and free; state 7 -> lift_upper (27.5, -32).
    About 90-110 m in all.
 5. END OF STATE 7 (0x1001ae13a ... 0x1001ae231): the helicopter enters the elevator slot and the RUNWAY IS FINALLY RELEASED (swap 0x80 <->
    0x7c at 0x1001ae183). The next helicopter cannot start its approach before that.
 6. Elevator down -> taxi from the bottom of the elevator to the centre of the hangar (27.5 m) -> the crane takes it -> the elevator
    rises at once (0x1001ad6a4 ..., 0x1001b1164).
Helicopters TAKE OFF directly from the platform (slot 0x84, 0x1001ae3c1); only landing uses the runway.
Wider fact: the runway (slot 0x80, with the single runway state 0x24, single path 0xf0, progress 0x108, speed 0x10c, single reserved
place 0x90 and single approach timer 0x110) is the real bottleneck of the carrier, not the elevator; the manager structure ends near
0x178 and the next object follows right away, so there is no room for a second slot without moving things.
A helicopter that waits too long (> 3600 ticks) is sent back to the queue like a failed approach.

Why you cannot move the touch point by a simple setting: the distance to mid-runway also drives the whole aircraft landing (descent
slope at 0x1001b1be6, thresholds 800/200/400 m): changing the constant breaks aircraft.


2. MODULES KEPT (validated in game, 2026-10-24) [GAME]
------------------------------------------------------
  helicos_approche_x2: the aim distance 50 -> 100 m (constant 100.0 read instead of 50.0 at 0x1001ad3ef): ~10 m/s instead of ~5.
  helicos_pres_ascenseur: touch near the elevator (z ~ -40) instead of mid-runway:
      - aim point at most z = runway_start + 48 (-20) instead of + 120 (constant at 0x1001ad3fc, 120.0 -> 48.0)
      - gear-down / landing state within 70 m of mid-runway instead of 20 m (0x1001b1066, 20.0 -> 70.0)
      - target height = 0.5 x (-40 - z): a 21-byte cave that subtracts 23.5 before the -2.0 clamp, branched from 0x1001ad57a.

3. EXPERIMENT THAT FAILED: landing directly on the elevator platform (module helicos_ascenseur, source in workshop/outil/generateurs/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Idea: helicopters already TAKE OFF from the platform; make them LAND on it too, in an "elevator lane" (slot 0x84, free during returns),
so a helicopter lands on the platform while an aircraft uses the runway. Cave of 5.5 KB + 3 hooks (entry of returns mode 0x1001acd94,
exit 0x1001ad7af, taxi 6 -> 7 at 0x1001ae071). Planned behaviour: aim at lift_upper (27.5, 20, -32) turned like the platform
(objects are rotated 90 degrees there: m02 = -1, m20 = 1), hover ~15 m above while the platform is not up and free (slot 0x7c == -1 and
carrier+0x1518 ~ 0), skip the roll by jumping into the existing end of state 7 (0x1001ae13a), fall back to the runway if the
landing fails.
Result in game (2026-10-24): the first helicopter used the lane well (runway at 0:10, on the platform at 0:52, approach 0:42), but two
helicopters COLLIDED: the game logged destroy_vehicle of a Razorbill (type 12) with no shooter at 06:26:19 UTC. No game crash.
Probable cause (not verified): the one descending onto the platform (x = 27.5) and one waiting at lift_upper_wait (x = 12, 15.5 m away) with
their rotors. The game destroys vehicles that touch. The idea was dropped, the code is kept as reference. A variant with TWO landing places
on the deck (8/10) would not beat the lane.


4. SECOND ELEVATOR: STUDY AND VERDICT (not done, 9/10, not worth it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Inventory:
- rom_0/game_objects/vehicle_chassis_carrier.xml physical bodies: `hull` (collision meshes/aircraft_carrier/aircraft_carrier_hangar_physics.cphys, 234 KB),
  `lift` (meshes/chassis_carrier_lift_phys.cphys = ONE box x 21..33, y 19..20, z -45.5..-18.2: a 12 x 27 m platform) and `lift_personnel`.
  Constraints: `lift_constraint` (type 0, course -9..0) and `lift_personnel` (type 4); the `drive_*` settings do nothing (the code imposes the position).
- Meshes really used: aircraft_carrier_shell/aircraft_carrier_lift.mesh (platform), aircraft_carrier_hangar.mesh (deck + hangar), gantry_crane*.mesh (crane).
  chassis_carrier.mesh, chassis_carrier_phys.cphys, chassis_carrier_lift.mesh and chassis_carrier_crane* are NOT referenced by any file (leftovers).
- Navigation mesh `lift_aircraft` is attached to body n. 1 (the platform).
- Program: ONE elevator hard-coded in the carrier (position 0x1518, levels 0x1520, setpoint 0x1538, offset 0x152c, energy 0x1218, physics link 0x14e0 found
  by the name `lift_constraint` in ::build 0x100193022): a 2nd constraint in the XML would simply be ignored. ONE crane, ONE manager with ONE elevator slot (0x7c).
- Client side: the client does not receive the platform height in clear; it reads the crane / elevator states (e_crane_state, e_elevator_state, for the
  screens) in parse_state (0x10011b954) and the pieces' positions through the general body mechanism (get_body_transform). A 2nd platform would
  probably be shown to other players without client changes [UNV]; the ship screens would only show elevator 1.
Bricks and difficulty: hole in the deck + platform + well in the hangar (3D) 7/10; collisions 8/10 (477 PhysX cooked convex pieces to re-cut: no cooking
tool); body + constraint in the XML 3/10 but useless without code; paths 2/10; logic (two elevators, crane sharing, energy, save) 9/10; multiplayer 5/10.
Verdict: possible in theory, 8-12 sessions on Mac and again 3-5 on another platform, and the GAIN IS SMALL: the runway stays single, the elevator is not
what slows aircraft. Cheaper alternatives: x3 elevator (2/10, little gain), two aircraft on the platform (6/10).
The one real benefit (a path of their own for helicopters) is what the lane tried to give.
