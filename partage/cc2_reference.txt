====================================================================================================
Function: docking_manager_air::update, aircraft holding pattern (helicopters are not concerned).

about 3 km behind the ship), once per 12.3 km lap (154 s at 80 m/s).

redirected to the grant block at 0x1001af914; (b) the front turn is made on the same circle as the rear turn
(centre (-1000, -1800) in ship coordinates, radius 1200 m): lap 7.5 km / 94 s.
Measured: 4 aircraft stored in 7:28 game time, one runway grant every 1:31, 0 failed approaches (stock not measured on
the real game; the queue simulation in workshop/outil/generateurs/ estimates 154-158 s per aircraft stock vs 91 s measured).
